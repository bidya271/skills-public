# LinkedIn Post Writer

Write authentic LinkedIn posts that sound like a seasoned operator, not ChatGPT.

---

## The Problem
Generic AI writing is riddled with obvious tells: rocket emojis, hyperbolic metaphors (*"In today's fast-paced digital era"*), passive throat-clearing openings, and corporate fluff that hurts credibility.

## The Solution
This skill enforces strict anti-AI writing parameters:
1. **Ruthless Sentence Rhythm:** Short, punchy, spoken cadence.
2. **Banned Clichés:** Zero rocket emojis, zero "delighted to announce", zero generic buzzwords.
3. **Real Operating Hooks:** Starts directly inside a moment of tension or unexpected counter-intuitive observation.
