## The Problem

Generic AI writing is ruining personal branding.

Everyone can spot ChatGPT copy from a mile away. It fills feeds with obvious tells like rocket emojis hyperbolic metaphors like in todays fast paced digital world and passive throat clearing openings.

When founders and executives use standard AI tools to write posts they look inexperienced and out of touch. The copy sounds synthetic and corporate. Readers immediately scroll past because the content lacks personal tension tactical specificity and genuine human voice.

## The Solution

LinkedIn Post Writer acts as a ruthless ghostwriter and copy editor for serious operators.

It eliminates every generic AI cliché. It teaches your agent to think like an experienced founder who writes from personal scars hard won lessons and counter intuitive industry truths.

## How This Skill Solves It

* **Ruthless Spoken Cadence:** Enforces short punchy sentence rhythm that mimics natural human speech rather than robotic paragraphs.
* **Complete AI Tell Purge:** Automatically bans rocket emojis corporate buzzwords and artificial excitement like delighted to announce.
* **Instant Tension Hooks:** Opens directly inside a high stakes moment a vulnerable failure or a counter intuitive thesis.
* **High Retention Formatting:** Uses structured white space and single breath statements to maximize dwell time on mobile screens.
* **Tactical Operator Takeaways:** Delivers concrete actionable advice rather than vague motivational platitudes.
