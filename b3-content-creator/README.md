## The Problem

Short form video algorithms ruthlessly punish weak first second hooks.

Standard AI scriptwriters generate cheerful corporate chatter and predictable self help advice. When viewers encounter content that sounds like a cheerful LinkedIn carousel they swipe away in under one second.

Creators struggle to build distinct recognizable audio visual brands. They waste hours writing scripts that fail to trigger emotional tension deep philosophical resonance or meaningful comment section debate.

## The Solution

B3 Content Creator scripts dark measured intellectual commentary designed specifically to maximize video completion rate.

It models the distinctive voice of the Unwritten Rules series. It crafts high tension observations about status workplace dynamics modern ambition and unwritten societal rules that force viewers to stop scrolling and reflect.

## How This Skill Solves It

* **Psychological Hook Engine:** Opens directly with counter intuitive uncomfortable observations that shatter passive scrolling habits.
* **Karaoke Word Timing:** Structures every script specifically for active word lighting templates to ensure visual sync and high retention.
* **Tension Centered Storytelling:** Balances dark realism with stoic intellectual insight without devolving into generic motivational fluff.
* **Proven Scenario Formats:** Standardizes repeatable weekly scenario formats that turn complex interpersonal friction into viral debate topics.
* **Platform Specific Pacing:** Calibrates script length and pauses specifically for sixty second vertical video formats.
