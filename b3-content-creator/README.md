# B3 Content Creator

Produce dark, measured commentary and high-retention hybrid karaoke reels for short-form social video.

---

## The Problem
Short-form social algorithms punish weak first-second retention, while generic AI scriptwriters produce uninspired, cheerful corporate chatter that gets immediately skipped.

## The Solution
B3 Content Creator scripts high-tension, reflective content designed specifically for modern short-form video:
1. **Measured Hooks:** Grabs attention through counter-intuitive societal and professional truths.
2. **Karaoke Word Timing:** Formats script segments specifically for active-word lighting video templates.
3. **Weekly Scenario Formats:** Standardizes repeatable, viral commentary themes based on real-world dilemmas.
