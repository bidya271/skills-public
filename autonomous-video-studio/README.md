# Autonomous Video Studio

Turn scripts and concepts into 60fps cinematic animated videos and schedule them to social channels automatically.

---

## The Problem
Producing consistent video content requires juggling storyboard generation, multi-plane animation, video rendering, cloud asset staging, and manual social media scheduling.

## The Solution
Autonomous Video Studio unifies the entire pipeline into one agent capability:
1. **Storyboard & Illustration Engine:** Generates consistent 16:9 2D scenes and panels.
2. **Dense Motion Synthesizer:** Applies HTML5/Canvas keyframes and multi-plane parallax instead of static slideshows.
3. **Automated Buffer Dispatch:** Stages rendered MP4s and schedules posts directly via Buffer's GraphQL API.
