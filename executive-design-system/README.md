## The Problem

When engineers and solo founders build with Cursor or Claude the AI defaults to generic Tailwind templates.

It generates harsh purple gradients oversized cards bad vertical rhythm and visual clutter. Most AI generated interfaces look like student prototypes rather than credible business software.

Teams waste hundreds of hours manually tweaking padding borders and typography. Worse still they ship clunky enterprise dashboards that overwhelm users with raw tables and confusing modals. Hiring a senior product designer costs thousands of dollars while traditional UI libraries only give you dumb components with no design judgment.

## The Solution

Executive Design System injects high taste design engineering rules directly into your agent runtime.

It teaches your AI coding assistant to operate like a principal designer at Stripe Raycast or Linear. Instead of generating decorative filler it enforces information density visual hierarchy and calm ergonomics across your entire application.

## How This Skill Solves It

* **Three Tier Surface Architecture:** Organizes screens into Action Center for human approvals Activity Pulse for autonomous feeds and The Vault for deep storage instead of dumping users into raw tables.
* **Mad Lib Rule Builders:** Replaces raw JSON and cron configurations with tokenized human sentences that anyone can configure without errors.
* **Progressive Technical Disclosure:** Shows clean executive summaries by default and tucks deep technical payload details into expandable operator drawers.
* **Calm Motion Physics:** Implements Lenis smooth scroll and subtle micro interactions rather than jarring animations.
* **High Contrast Data Hierarchy:** Enforces strict typography scale dark mode contrast tokens and zero friction keyboard shortcuts.
