# Internal Communications Engine

Draft high-impact executive updates 3P team reports post-mortem incident analyses and company newsletters with boardroom polish.

You can buy the full skill pack for the price of a coffee directly on the listing page.

## The Problem

Most internal corporate communication is bloated vague and ignored.

Leaders and managers write dense unstructured status emails that bury critical blockers. Team updates take hours to write yet executives finish reading them without knowing whether a project is on track at risk or blocked.

When incidents happen post-mortems either devolve into defensive finger-pointing or fail to extract actionable systemic preventions.

## The Solution

This skill equips your agent with battle-tested internal communication templates modeled after high-performance engineering cultures like Amazon Stripe and Google.

It transforms messy status notes into crisp 3P reports (Progress Plans Problems) structured leadership briefs and transparent blameless incident reviews.

## How This Skill Solves It

* **3P Progress Plans Problems Framework:** Forces concise bulleted accountability across weekly team cycles.
* **Executive Leadership Summaries:** Surfaces headline metrics high-severity risks and required executive decisions upfront.
* **Blameless Incident Post-Mortems:** Analyzes root causes timeline chronologies customer impact and durable remediation items.
* **Company Newsletters & FAQs:** Translates complex strategic pivots into accessible transparent company-wide communications.
